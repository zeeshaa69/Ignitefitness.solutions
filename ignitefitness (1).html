Questions

FAQs

Do I have to give up my favourite foods? +
Absolutely not. Shahbaz's entire approach is built around working with the foods you love, not against them. You'll learn how to include them in a way that still produces incredible fat loss results.
How quickly will I see results? +
Most clients start seeing noticeable changes within the first 2–3 weeks. Significant results of 30–40 lbs typically occur over 12–16 weeks, though this varies based on your starting point and consistency.
I'm super busy. Can I still do this? +
Yes! The program is designed for real life. Plans are tailored to your schedule and time availability — whether you have 20 minutes or an hour. Many of our most successful clients are busy professionals and parents.
Do I need a gym membership? +
Not at all. Shahbaz creates plans for home workouts, gym workouts, or a combination of both — whatever works best for your situation and preferences.
Is this just for men or women too? +
The program is open to both men and women. Shahbaz has successfully coached hundreds of clients of all genders, ages, and fitness backgrounds.
How do I get started? +
Simply click the "Book Your Free Call" button above or below to schedule a free 30-minute consultation call with Shahbaz to discuss your goals and see if the program is right for you.
